read width:int height:int leagueLevel:int
read mySanctumX:int mySanctumY:int oppSanctumX:int oppSanctumY:int
gameloop
read gems:int mySanctumHp:int oppSanctumHp:int
loop height read row:word(16)
read pentagramCount:int
loop pentagramCount read pentagramId:int px:int py:int owner:int
read minionCount:int
loop minionCount read minionId:int minionOwner:int type:word(10) x:int y:int hp:int
write join("SUMMON", "0", "HOUND")

STATEMENT
Channel Nerdna's dark magic! Spend your Energy Gems each turn to summon Hounds, Guards, and Wraiths on controlled Pentagrams and activate your legions to crush the rival Overlord's Sanctum.

INPUT
width: Width of the labyrinth (11)
height: Height of the labyrinth (11)
leagueLevel: Current league level (1=Wood 2, 2=Wood 1, 3=Bronze+)
mySanctumX: X coordinate of your Overlord Sanctum
mySanctumY: Y coordinate of your Overlord Sanctum
oppSanctumX: X coordinate of opponent Sanctum
oppSanctumY: Y coordinate of opponent Sanctum
gems: Energy Gems available to spend this turn
mySanctumHp: Remaining HP of your Sanctum
oppSanctumHp: Remaining HP of opponent Sanctum
row: One row of the thin-wall labyrinth as uppercase hex digits (0-F: 1=North, 2=East, 4=South, 8=West)
pentagramCount: Number of Summoning Pentagrams
pentagramId: Unique ID of the Pentagram
px: X coordinate of the Pentagram
py: Y coordinate of the Pentagram
owner: -1=neutral, 0=yours, 1=opponent
minionCount: Total number of active minions
minionId: Unique ID of the minion
minionOwner: 0=yours (listed first), 1=opponent
type: Minion class (HOUND, GUARD, WRAITH)
x: X coordinate of the minion
y: Y coordinate of the minion
hp: Remaining HP of the minion

OUTPUT
A single line of semicolon-separated commands: SUMMON <pentagramId> <HOUND|GUARD|WRAITH> ; ACTIVATE <minionId> <targetX> <targetY>
